## 1. The "Competitor AI Omission" 3-Minute Loom Teardown (Highest Conversion)

### How to Prepare:
1. Run their domain in OmniGEO (takes 15 seconds).
2. Open their website and open Perplexity / ChatGPT Search on a second tab.
3. Record your screen with Loom (keep it strictly between 2:30 and 3:30 minutes).

### Word-for-Word Video Script:

```text
"Hey [First Name],

I was doing some research on the top software platforms in the [Niche/Category] space, and I noticed something alarming regarding how AI search engines are routing your prospective buyers.

[Switch tab to Perplexity / ChatGPT]

Right here, I prompted ChatGPT Search and Perplexity with the #1 buying intent prompt your prospects ask when evaluating solutions: 'What are the best [Niche] tools for scaling teams in 2026?'

Look at the results: [Competitor A] and [Competitor B] are recommended at the very top with direct clickable citation links and excerpt summaries pulling from G2 and Reddit.

Your brand, [Target Company], is completely omitted. Zero mentions, zero citations.

[Switch tab to OmniGEO Audit Report]

I ran your domain through our Generative Engine Optimization analyzer, and here is why this is happening:

First, your robots.txt is currently restricting [or missing explicit rules for] PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot, which blocks real-time citation crawlers.

Second, your competitors have established high-weight semantic entity nodes on Wikidata and Reddit, while your website is missing structured JSON-LD Organization markup and an /llms.txt standard file.

Because of this, we calculate that approximately [X]% of commercial AI discovery queries in your niche are currently being captured by [Competitor A], representing an estimated $[Y]k in pipeline at risk.

We’ve mapped out the exact 30-day technical and citation sprint to fix this and get your brand into the LLM recommendation context window.

If you’d like me to send over the full interactive audit and the code snippets to patch this, let me know. Happy to send it over free of charge."
```
