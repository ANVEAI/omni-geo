# 05. The GEO Executive Sales Presentation & Pitch Framework

> **Format**: 10-Slide Zoom Presentation  
> **Goal**: Close a $2,500 One-Time Audit or $5,000/mo Retainer in a 25-minute call.

---

### Slide 1: The Title
* **Headline**: Capturing the Generative Shift: Why AI Search is Deciding Your Next Customers
* **Sub-headline**: How [Target Company] Can Win the Recommendation Battle in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.
* **Speaker Note**: "Thank you for jumping on. Today we aren't talking about traditional 10 blue links on Google. We are talking about how prospective buyers actually make decisions today using generative AI."

---

### Slide 2: The Macro Shift (The 'Burning Platform')
* **Headline**: The Death of the Traditional Click
* **Key Points**:
  - Gartner predicts organic search traffic to brand websites will fall by 25%+ as AI engines provide direct answers.
  - 65%+ of B2B software evaluators now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude: *"What is the best tool for X?"*
  - If you aren't recommended in that first generated response, you do not exist to that buyer.
* **Speaker Note**: "When a buyer asks an LLM for recommendations, the model only recommends 2 to 3 vendors. If your competitor is there and you aren't, you lose the deal before you ever get a demo request."

---

### Slide 3: The Current Reality (The Diagnosis)
* **Visual**: Screenshot of live Perplexity or ChatGPT prompt showing competitors recommended and client missing.
* **Headline**: What Happens When Your Prospects Search Today
* **Data Point**: [Competitor A] recommended in 85% of queries; [Client] omitted in 80% of queries.
* **Speaker Note**: "Here is what happens when someone asks for top tools in your category right now. Notice how Competitor A is cited directly, pulling from Reddit and G2. Your team isn't mentioned."

---

### Slide 4: The Financial Bleed (Cost of Inaction)
* **Headline**: Quantifying the Competitor Leakage Rate
* **Key Metric**: Estimated $[X] annually in pipeline diverted to alternatives.
* **Explanation**: Based on an estimated 4,000 high-intent monthly AI buying prompts in your sector and your current omission rate.
* **Speaker Note**: "This isn't an academic SEO problem. This is a direct pipeline leak. Every week you remain invisible to LLMs, qualified inbounds are being routed to your competitors."

---

### Slide 5: The Anatomy of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
* **Headline**: How AI Models Decide Who to Recommend
* **Visual**: The 4 Pillars diagram:
  1. **Generative Share of Voice (35%)**: Prompt frequency & placement.
  2. **Citation Authority (25%)**: Reddit, G2, Wikipedia, Wikidata.
  3. **Technical Crawlability (20%)**: robots.txt bot rules and /llms.txt standard.
  4. **Semantic Architecture (20%)**: JSON-LD Knowledge Graph & comparison tables.
* **Speaker Note**: "LLMs don't read the web like Google did. They look for consensus across verified knowledge graphs, unblocked AI crawlers, and authoritative community discussions."

---

### Slide 6: The Technical Fix (The Low-Hanging Fruit)
* **Headline**: Fixing Machine-Readability First
* **Key Points**:
  - Unblocking GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot.
  - Deploying an official `/llms.txt` file (the new markdown standard for AI).
  - Injecting Organization & Product JSON-LD with entity linkages.
* **Speaker Note**: "The good news is that over 50% of the problem is technical plumbing that we can fix in the first 7 days."

---

### Slide 7: The Authority Network (Winning the Citations)
* **Headline**: Seeding the Knowledge Nodes That Fuel LLMs
* **Key Points**:
  - Establishing a verified Wikidata entity.
  - Seeding high-signal Reddit technical discussions (where Perplexity and OpenAI buy training data).
  - Review platform alignment (G2/Capterra).
* **Speaker Note**: "Both Google and OpenAI have formal data-licensing partnerships with Reddit. By participating authoritatively in the right discussions, we feed the exact data engines that recommend products."

---

### Slide 8: The 30-Day Implementation Sprint
* **Headline**: From Omission to Dominance in 4 Weeks
* **Timeline Table**:
  - **Week 1**: Technical Bot Whitelisting, /llms.txt & Knowledge Graph.
  - **Week 2**: Wikidata Entity creation & Reddit citation campaign.
  - **Week 3**: Answer-First content rewrite & HTML comparison matrices.
  - **Week 4**: Multi-Model benchmark verification and SOV tracking.

---

### Slide 9: Engagement Options
* **Headline**: How We Can Partner
* **Column 1: Complete Audit & Technical Package ($2,500 One-Time)**
  - Full multi-model audit, all custom code assets, and 60-min executive briefing.
* **Column 2: Full-Service GEO Remediation Retainer ($5,000 / Month)**
  - Full hands-off agency execution across engineering, citation seeding, content architecture, and weekly drift monitoring.

---

### Slide 10: Next Steps
* **Headline**: Ready to Close the Gap?
* **Call to Action**:
  - "Shall we begin with Option A to deliver the complete technical blueprint, or would you prefer our team to handle the full execution under Option B?"
* **Speaker Note**: [Stop talking. Let the client respond first.]
