### 1. Scope of Services & Objectives
Consultant agrees to provide Generative Engine Optimization ("GEO") consulting and implementation services designed to enhance Client's brand visibility, citation indexation, and recommendation frequency across generative artificial intelligence platforms including OpenAI SearchGPT, Perplexity AI, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude.

#### Key Service Deliverables:
1. **Technical Foundation**:
   - Audit and configuration of `robots.txt` AI crawler directives.
   - Generation and validation of `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` standard files.
   - Structured JSON-LD Knowledge Graph creation and sameAs entity linkage.
2. **Authority & Citation Network Optimization**:
   - Analysis of external citation platforms including Reddit, Wikidata, Crunchbase, and G2.
   - Strategy and execution guidelines for authoritative community citations.
3. **Content Structuring & Comparison Architecture**:
   - Content optimization recommendations adopting the "Answer-First" inverted pyramid layout.
   - Design of semantic competitor comparison tables for scraper extraction.
4. **Benchmarking & Reporting**:
   - Multi-model evaluation reporting measuring Share of Voice (SOV) and recommendation rank.
